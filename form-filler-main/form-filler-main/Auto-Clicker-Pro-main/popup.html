    How to Use Form Filler Pro

    1
    Record Form Actions

    Click Record. On your webpage, click any dropdown to open it, select your desired option, click checkboxes/buttons, and fill static fields. Click Finish on the floating banner or in the popup when done.

    2
    Designate the Paragraph Field

    In the Steps list, find your message/comment/detail text area and click 📄 Set as Para Box. This tells the extension to fill it with different paragraphs each rotation.

    3
    Load Your TXT File

    Click Load TXT or Paste. Each paragraph in the file will be used for 1 rotation. After being used, it is removed from the queue.

    4
    Set Rotations & Run

    Click ⚡ Match TXT to match the loop count to your remaining paragraphs, then hit Start Sequence! When done, you can click Save TXT to download the remaining unused content.

    Start Recording

    Interactions on the page will be recorded as steps:
    • Checkboxes & Options: Click checkboxes, radio buttons or dropdown items.
    • Text Fields: Click and type normal info (Name, Email, etc.).
    • Buttons: Click submit or navigation buttons.

    Click Finish on the floating banner or Stop in the popup when finished.

    Add Condition Step

    Conditions let you check if something on the page matches — then perform different clicks.
